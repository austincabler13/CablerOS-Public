# CablerOS Repository & Service Rules — DRAFT

**Status:** Draft  
**Draft date:** September 29, 2026

These rules govern CablerOS-operated package repositories, APIs, update services, cloud services, developer services, and similar infrastructure. They are separate from the CablerOS software license.

## 1. General Access

Third-party software and compatible CablerOS-derived projects may use official services by default when they comply with published service rules.

Service access does not create ownership of CablerOS and does not grant broader software-license rights.

## 2. Compatibility Claims

Claims such as "Compatible with CablerOS" must be accurate and non-misleading.

Third parties may not imply official status, sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or affiliation that has not actually been granted.

## 3. Update Infrastructure

Compatible unofficial projects may use official CablerOS update infrastructure where technically supported.

CablerOS may restrict or block such access when a project:

- becomes incompatible;
- causes a security problem;
- generates abusive or harmful load;
- interferes with service reliability; or
- violates applicable service rules.

## 4. APIs and Developer Services

CablerOS may require authentication, rate limits, quotas, technical controls, or other reasonable service requirements.

Abuse of a service may result in suspension of service access without automatically terminating CablerOS software-license rights.

## 5. Package Repository

CablerOS may remove or disable third-party packages that:

- violate repository rules;
- create security risks;
- materially disrupt repository operation;
- are technically broken in a way that harms users or infrastructure; or
- otherwise fail published repository requirements.

Developers may request review or appeal of package-removal decisions.

For an active security threat, CablerOS may remove a package immediately and process the appeal afterward.

## 6. No Guaranteed Hosting or Support

Publication in an official repository does not guarantee permanent hosting.

Unofficial forks and third-party packages do not receive guaranteed CablerOS support. CablerOS may voluntarily provide assistance without creating an ongoing obligation.
